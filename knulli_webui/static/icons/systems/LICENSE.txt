System icons
============

Retro game console icons by KyleBing
https://github.com/KyleBing/retro-game-console-icons
Licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: icons taken from series_trimui/300w@1x and series_miyoo/132w@1x,
cropped to their visible area and resized to fit 128x128 pixels.
They are generated by tools/build_icons.py, which also writes map.json
(Knulli/Batocera system folder -> icon file).
