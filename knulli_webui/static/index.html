      Protect Knulli WebUI

      Anyone on your network can open this page and delete ROMs, saves and screenshots. Set a username and password: they are saved in config.json and asked by the browser from the next login on.

      Delete ROMs?

        This cannot be undone. The entries will also be removed from gamelist.xml (a .bak copy is saved).
